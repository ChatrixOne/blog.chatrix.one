Monal is a free, open-source XMPP chat app for iPhone and iPad. This guide walks you through everything: creating an account on Chatrix.One, setting up Monal, and sending your first message. Takes about 5 minutes.

---

## Step 1 - Go to chatrix.one and start registration

Open your browser, go to **[chatrix.one](https://chatrix.one)** and tap **Create an account**. You will be asked to confirm you are human — tick the **I am human** box and tap **Continue**.

## Step 5 - Register your account

You are now on the Welcome screen. Tap **Register a new account**. The server field is already set to **chatrix.one**. Pick a username and a strong password, then tap **Register with chatrix.one**.

A **Success!** popup confirms your new address, something like `yourname@chatrix.one`. Tap **Close**.

---

## Step 6 - Add a contact and start chatting

You land on the **Chats** screen. Tap the contacts icon (top right), then the **+** button to add someone. Type their XMPP address in the format `name@server.tld` and tap **Add**. A contact request is sent and once they accept it, you are ready to chat.
